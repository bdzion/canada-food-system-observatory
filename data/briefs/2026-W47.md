## Questions to ask

- There is more grain in storage than in any recent year at this time. Is that a comfortable reserve, or grain waiting for trains and ships?
- Wholesale prices for strawberries from the United States and peppers from Ontario are higher than in any recent year at this time. Is the cause weather where they are grown, the exchange rate, or something else?
- Fewer ships than usual are passing through the Strait of Hormuz and the Bab el-Mandeb Strait. Which Canadian imports and exports use these routes, and what are the alternatives?
- Diesel costs more than at this time in any recent year, after inflation. How much of that reaches farm costs and grocery prices, and how soon?
